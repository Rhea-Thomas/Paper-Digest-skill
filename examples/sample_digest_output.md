# Efficient Retrieval for Small Language Models

**Authors:** Not identifiable in sample text
**Source:** Not identifiable in sample text

## Problem
The paper investigates whether small retrieval-augmented models can match the
performance of much larger language models on question-answering benchmarks,
which matters for reducing inference cost.

## Method
The authors combine a lightweight dense retriever with a 1-billion-parameter
generator model, evaluating the pipeline on the NaturalQuestions benchmark.

## Key Results
- Achieves 62.3 Exact Match (EM) on NaturalQuestions
- Comes within 2 points of a 7B-parameter baseline
- Uses roughly 6x less compute than that baseline

## Limitations
Evaluation is limited to English text, and the paper does not test multi-hop
reasoning benchmarks.
